## 🌟 THE IDEA

This project is more than a simple coffee shop website.

It is an attempt to capture the feeling of walking into your favourite café —  
the aroma of freshly brewed coffee, the warmth of the atmosphere,  
and that peaceful moment when everything else fades away.

Every part of this project was created with one thought:

> **Make something people don't just visit — make something they remember.**
