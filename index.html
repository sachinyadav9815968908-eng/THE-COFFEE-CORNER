About Us

Nestled in the heart of the UCOE Basement at Punjabi University, our coffee house is the new go-to spot for students and faculty. We're dedicated to serving up exceptional coffee in a vibrant, welcoming space. Whether you're prepping for an exam or taking a well-deserved break, we're here to fuel your day with great brews, tasty snacks, and a friendly atmosphere.
